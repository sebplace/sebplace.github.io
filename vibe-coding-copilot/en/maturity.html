Institutional maturity diagnostic

A short, honest self-check that points you to the next useful page — not a fake certified score.

What this diagnostic does It helps you choose a realistic next step based on your GitHub starting point, your first Copilot usage, and your current level of internal alignment.

Answer the six questions below. The final recommendation will point you to First commit, Scenarios, or Plans & reality depending on your current context.

1

Where are you today on the GitHub side?

2

How many people already use Copilot, even informally?

3

Is there already a budget line or explicit mandate behind this topic?

4

Your main need today is mostly…

5

Do you still need concrete examples to build internal buy-in?

6

Are your documents, procedures, or trusted data already structured enough to guide Copilot?
